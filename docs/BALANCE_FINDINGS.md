# Balance findings (section 4.7)

> **Status:** the changes recommended here were applied. See `docs/BALANCE_CHANGES.md` for what changed and the after-numbers; this file is the diagnosis of the original game.

Source: `docs/BALANCE_REPORT.md`, generated by `npm run sim:balance` (17,580 duels on the real engine and AI, none stuck).
What-if tuning: `docs/BALANCE_GATHER_WHATIF.md`.

**How far to trust this.** Both sides are played by the game's own AI, so every number says how a power behaves *under that AI*, not under a human. Intervals are 95%. Nothing below assumes who "should" be strong.

## The one real problem: Gather Soul is cheaper than playing a follower round

- The round leader wins **69%** of rounds, on every call. Leadership alternates, so it does not make a seat unfair, but half of each duel is spent as the likely loser.
- A lost round costs about **11.7 HP** (and returns +3 Soul); a won round gives about +2.6 HP. A follower's expected round is therefore roughly -7 HP, +2 Soul, plus the card's own Soul cost.
- Gather Soul costs **5 HP** and gives **+4 Soul**, with no card spent. It is better than playing most follower rounds.
- This is why "always gather" wins. It also means the new sacrifice presentation (4.6) is describing something that is, mechanically, still a good deal.

Tuning sweep (always-gather vs the normal AI, 500 duels each, never-gather stays near 50%):

| Gather cost | Always-gather win rate |
|---|---|
| -5 HP / +4 Soul / cooldown 3 (current) | 70.8% |
| -8 HP | 61.8% |
| -10 HP | 56.4% |
| **-12 HP** | **51.2%** |
| -5 HP, cooldown 5 | 65.8% |
| -8 HP, cooldown 5 | 63.8% |
| -8 HP / +3 Soul | 63.4% |

Raising the HP cost works; cooldown and Soul changes alone do not. About **-10 to -12 HP** removes the dominance while keeping Gather Soul a real option. The other lever is the leader edge itself (69% is large), which is a deeper design question and worth its own look before changing the numbers.

## Caveats

- AI quality: the AI's own Gather Soul use is neutral, and its POWER-heavy calling is not optimal, so some "strategy" gaps are AI weakness. A human would find them faster.
- Power timing: every power fires once per duel. Insight and the round-1 Wish powers fire at round 1-1.6, which may be wasted. Their low scores are suspect until the AI times them better.
- Step progression adds only a few points (about +2 to +4 on average), so Steps are gentle.
- Each round-robin pairing has only 12 games. Treat lopsided pairings (e.g. Izanami over Tiamat 11/12) as leads to re-run, not conclusions.
