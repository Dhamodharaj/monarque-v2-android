# Balance diagnostics

Generated by `scripts/sim-balance.mjs`. 17580 duels on the real engine with the real AI; 0 unfinished.

**Read this first.** These are diagnostic signals, not player-balance measurements. Both sides are played by the game's own AI, so a result describes a power *as that AI uses it*. A power the AI under-uses will look weak here and may be strong in a human's hands (see the "fired in" and "1st use" columns). Seats are balanced in every power comparison, so seat bias cancels. Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals. Nothing in this report assumes which character *should* be strong.

Seat 1 win rate by how long the duel lasted: ends on an odd round 49.6% (46%–53%), on an even round 48.8% (45%–52%). Seat 1 leads the odd rounds, so a gap here is the leader edge showing up as a parity effect.

Does any power make the seat matter? Seat 1 win rate across the 32 contenders' mirrors has a spread of ±14.0% (one s.d.); pure chance with 14 duels each would give ±13.4%. No contender is seat-sensitive beyond chance.
